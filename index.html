01 / UNDERSTANDING DISASTERS

Disasters affect people, places and communities.

A disaster occurs when a hazardous event interacts with exposed and vulnerable people or systems. Understanding hazards and preparing in advance can reduce their impact.

02 / TYPES OF DISASTERS

Know the hazards.

India faces different natural and human-made hazards across its diverse geographical regions.

01

Cyclone

Strong winds, heavy rainfall and coastal storm surges can affect coastal communities.

02

Flood

Flooding may occur due to intense rainfall, river overflow and inadequate drainage.

03

Earthquake

Sudden ground movement can damage buildings, infrastructure and essential services.

04

Tsunami

Large sea waves can be triggered by undersea earthquakes and landslides.

05

Fire

Uncontrolled fires can threaten people, buildings, forests and infrastructure.

06

Industrial Disaster

Industrial accidents may involve hazardous materials, explosions or chemical releases.

03 / MANAGEMENT FRAMEWORK

The disaster management cycle.

Disaster management is a continuous process that begins before an event and continues after it.

01

Prevention

Avoid creating new risks through safer planning.

02

Mitigation

Reduce possible disaster impacts.

03

Preparedness

Plan, train and prepare resources.

04

Response

Protect lives and meet urgent needs.

05

Recovery

Restore services and rebuild safely.

06 / INDIA'S RESPONSE SYSTEM

Who responds during disasters?

01

NDMA

National Disaster Management Authority – national-level disaster management policies and guidance.

02

NDRF

National Disaster Response Force – specialised rescue and response operations.

03

SDMA

State Disaster Management Authority – state-level planning and coordination.

04

Local Authorities

Local administration supports warnings, evacuation, relief and coordination.

05

NGOs

Non-governmental organisations support relief, communities and recovery.

06

Volunteers

Trained community volunteers can support awareness and local emergency activities.

07 / TECHNOLOGY

Technology for disaster resilience.

Modern technology helps detect hazards, communicate warnings, map affected areas and support rescue teams.

GIS

Maps hazards, population and essential infrastructure.

Satellites

Supports large-area monitoring, mapping and forecasting.

Drones

Help survey difficult and affected locations.

IoT

Connected sensors can monitor infrastructure and conditions.

Early Warning

Alerts communities so protective action can begin early.

AI & Data

Data analysis can support risk assessment and decisions.
